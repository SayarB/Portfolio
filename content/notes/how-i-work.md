# How I work

## What I'm like to work with
- I'm passionate about tech, and I want to try every new thing and build new products.
- I help others as much as I can, and I try to be available and eager to help.
- I don't watch the clock. I'll go the extra mile when it matters.
- I care about keeping our tech as debt-free as possible, and I always push for that.
- When a new technology comes out, I'm keen to dive in, try it, and report back on what we could do with it.

## How I solve problems
I don't think of an entire spec at once. I'm much better at solving problems when I'm given one. So I like to work through problems one at a time, come up with my own solutions, and then build them. I'd rather get the potential problems out before I start, instead of building from assumptions and then being told to change the whole thing.

## What people get wrong about me at first
- That I won't help them with something. I will.
- That I just agree with whoever's in charge and have no opinions of my own about tech. I do have opinions.
- That I only understand frontend. I've worked across frontend and backend.
- That I don't want to learn because I hold opinions. I do want to learn. I just want to learn it from someone who can prove me wrong.

## Hard parts I've handled
- Pressure to ship fast with AI while guardrails and testing lag behind.
- Leading one person while still delivering my own work.
