# Who I am

## The short version
I'm a normal guy who's nerdy about computer stuff, music, and learning how everything in technology works. I get paid to build software for companies, and I studied it in college. I love making software, and I like coming up with solutions to problems that can be solved with tech.

## How I got into software
My school taught Java from the 8th standard. As soon as I learnt it, I was hooked and wanted to build applications with it. I picked up a little JFrame and was amazed that I could make buttons and windows.

I played a lot of games, so next I wanted to build them. As I learnt more programming I started thinking about how games were made, and that led me to Unity and C#, because C# felt syntactically close to Java.

From there I somehow ended up in React and frontend websites, then backend APIs. Then I just fell in love with it.

## The last two years
I learnt a lot about how a growing startup works. I loved the speed, and the sheer amount of things I had to understand and implement. The last year in particular, following the AI boom and making it work inside our product, has been great: new standards, new workflows, new environments, and the speed they unlock. I've loved the process of learning, and being able to work directly on AI in our company.

The hard part is the flip side of the boom: unrealistic expectations, and everybody banking heavily on AI and expecting me to as well. If implementation is no longer the bottleneck, testing should be, and pushing code without enough guardrails and checks has been daunting.

I also took on extra responsibility as a team lead with one person under me. Training them and getting work done together taught me a lot, and it pushed me to work even harder.
